# multi-condition-cci-comparison

## Summary

Compares pre-computed ligand-receptor cell-cell interaction (CCI) datasets across multiple conditions (e.g., control, moderate, critical COVID-19) in InterCellar's multiple-conditions module to highlight per-cell-type and per-interaction-pair patterns uniquely found in each condition.

## When to use

Use when two or more pre-computed CCI datasets (e.g., CellPhoneDB- or CellChat-derived) covering distinct biological conditions are available and the goal is to identify cell types or int-pairs whose communication is condition-specific, such as gain of interactions in critical vs moderate COVID-19.

## When NOT to use

- You only have a single condition/dataset and no multi-condition comparison is intended — InterCellar's comparison module requires multiple datasets to extract condition-unique patterns.
- Raw scRNA-seq count matrices without pre-computed ligand-receptor interactions — InterCellar builds on pre-computed, inferred interactions and does not infer CCI itself.
- The input CCI data was generated by a tool outside InterCellar's supported set (CellChat, CellPhoneDB, ICELLNET, SingleCellSignalR) — the article notes extension to other published tools is planned future work.

## Inputs

- Pre-computed ligand-receptor interaction CCI datasets, one per condition (e.g., three COVID-19 datasets from CellPhoneDB or CellChat)
- Cell cluster annotations per dataset (e.g., MC, NKT-p, MoMa, nrMa, CTL, Treg, ciliated, secretory, secretory-diff, neutrophils)
- InterCellar package (R >= 4.1, Bioconductor 3.14) or bioconda/Docker installation

## Outputs

- Back-to-back barplot of total interactions per cell cluster between conditions (back_to_back_barplot.png)
- Radar plots overlaying relative interaction numbers per viewpoint cell type across conditions
- Gene-verse dot plots of interaction scores for filtered int-pairs/cluster-pairs, one per condition
- Pie charts / proportion tables of condition-unique int-pair/cluster-pair couplets (e.g., 47% epithelial and 57% immune CXCL couplets unique to critical)
- Sunburst plot of significantly enriched functional terms per condition with p < 0.05 (1000 permutations) and exported results table

## How to apply

Upload each condition's CCI dataset into the InterCellar Shiny app (launched with InterCellar::run_app(reproducible = TRUE)) and use the multiple-condition comparison functionalities. In the cluster-verse, aggregate interaction counts per cluster and render back-to-back barplots juxtaposing per-cluster interaction counts between conditions (e.g., critical vs moderate COVID-19), or compute per-viewpoint relative interaction numbers and overlay them as radar plots across conditions. In the gene-verse, use interactive filtering to subset int-pairs (e.g., to CXCL1/CXCL3/CXCL6/CXCL16/CXCL17 chemokine genes) and specific cluster-pairs (e.g., secretory/secretory-diff to neutrophils), then compare dot plots across conditions to find patterns uniquely found in each condition. In the function-verse, extract condition-unique int-pairs and apply a one-sided permutation test with 1000 permutations, calling terms significant at p < 0.05 (e.g., 'inflammation mediated by chemokine and cytokine signaling pathway' significant in critical cases). Export figures and result tables using InterCellar's download options for reproducibility.

## Evaluation signals

- Back-to-back barplot reproduces the reported per-cluster count pattern: gain of interactions for MC and NKT-p in critical vs moderate, higher macrophage and lower T cell counts in critical.
- Radar plots show a consistent critical-case pattern with higher interaction numbers between all cell types (MoMa, nrMa, CTL, Treg, ciliated, secretory) and MC or NKT-p.
- Cross-condition dot plots confirm chemokine-driven secretory/secretory-diff to neutrophil communication is enriched in disease conditions and absent in healthy controls.
- Unique-couplet proportions match reported values: 47% epithelial and 57% immune CXCL couplets unique to critical; 56% macrophage, 41% NK, 20% T cell favoring critical.
- Permutation test (1000 permutations) yields 'inflammation mediated by chemokine and cytokine signaling pathway' significant (p < 0.05) in critical and 'T cell costimulation' significant in moderate, with CD160 & TNFRSF14 unique to moderate.

## Limitations

- InterCellar supports only a limited set of input tools (CellChat, CellPhoneDB, ICELLNET, SingleCellSignalR); extending input options to other published tools is planned future work.
- Predicted interactions await experimental validation, e.g., by single-molecule fluorescence in situ hybridization or flow cytometry co-occurrence.
- The one-sided permutation test on condition-unique int-pairs requires sufficient condition-unique interactions; results depend on the underlying prediction tool's reference database and statistical assumptions.
