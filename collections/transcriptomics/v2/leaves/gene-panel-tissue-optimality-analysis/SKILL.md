# gene-panel-tissue-optimality-analysis

## Summary

Rank the four GTEx-derived biosamples (whole blood, LCLs, skeletal muscle, fibroblasts) by their proportion of low-MRSD (<100 million reads) genes within each PanelApp disease gene panel, to identify the optimal RNA source per panel.

## When to use

Decision support for choosing the best RNA-seq biosample for a disease gene panel: you have per-tissue MRSD scores (*.results.mrsd.txt) and a panel gene list, and need the tissue minimizing required sequencing depth.

## When NOT to use

- The RNA-seq workflow deviates from bulk short-read poly-A enrichment RNA-seq processed with the Cummings-style pipeline — the GTEx-derived MRSD scores are not calibrated for long-read, single-cell, or spatially resolved RNA-seq
- The goal is to predict suitability for detecting allele-specific expression biases or differential gene expression — the MRSD model is not validated for these applications
- Disease involves widespread transcriptomic changes (e.g., interferonopathies, chromatin structure disorders) — MRSD predictions may not reflect coverage when genomic background alters expression, and eQTLs/sQTLs are not explicitly modeled

## Inputs

- Per-biosample MRSD score tables (*.results.mrsd.txt) for whole blood, LCLs, skeletal muscle and fibroblasts (GTEx-derived, MRSD in millions of reads)
- PanelApp disease gene panel gene lists (295 panels)
- List of multi-exon disease genes (3,322 genes, GENCODE v19 symbols and/or Ensembl IDs)

## Outputs

- Per-gene binary low-MRSD (<100M reads) classification matrix across the four biosamples
- Per-panel optimal biosample table (panel_optimal_tissue.tsv)
- Per-biosample counts of panels for which each tissue is optimal (e.g., fibroblasts 186/295 = 63.1%)

## How to apply

Load the per-tissue MRSD score tables (*.results.mrsd.txt, generated by MRSDexplorer) for whole blood, LCLs, skeletal muscle and fibroblasts with pandas, parsing MRSD values reported in millions of reads. Classify each of the 3,322 multi-exon disease genes as low-MRSD (<100 million reads) per tissue, treating '-' entries (median splice junction coverage of 0 reads) as NOT low-MRSD. Aggregate per gene across the four tissues and verify the fraction of genes low-MRSD in at least one biosample matches the reported 64.2% (2,133/3,322) as a sanity check. Load the 295 PanelApp disease gene panel gene lists, intersect each panel with the per-tissue low-MRSD classifications, compute each biosample's proportion of low-MRSD panel genes, and select the highest (or joint-highest) proportion as the optimal RNA source. Summarize per-biosample panel counts and write the per-panel optimal-biosample table to panel_optimal_tissue.tsv. The rationale is that a gene's MRSD is gene- and tissue-specific, so panels are best assayed in the tissue where most panel genes need <100M reads.

## Evaluation signals

- Fraction of the 3,322 multi-exon genes low-MRSD in at least one of the four biosamples equals the reported 64.2% (2,133/3,322)
- Fibroblasts are optimal (or joint-optimal) for 186/295 panels (63.1%), with the ranking fibroblasts (186) > LCLs (126) > skeletal muscle (70) > whole blood (21)
- '-' entries (median splice junction coverage of 0 reads) are excluded from the low-MRSD class rather than counted as low
- panel_optimal_tissue.tsv contains exactly 295 rows, one optimal biosample per PanelApp panel, with joint ties handled as joint-optimal
- MRSD values parsed as millions of reads against the <100M threshold, matching the *_results.mrsd.txt output schema

## Limitations

- MRSD validated only for bulk short-read poly-A enrichment RNA-seq processed with a specific bioinformatics pipeline; other experimental/bioinformatics approaches are not yet characterized
- The current MRSD model does not explicitly account for eQTLs or sQTLs, which influence gene expression profiles
- MRSD predictions may not accurately reflect coverage for disorders associated with widespread transcriptomic changes (e.g., interferonopathies, spliceosome disruption)
- Suitability of low-MRSD genes for differential expression and isoform imbalance investigations is likely but not quantified or proven
