# lasso-penalized-cox-model-building

## Summary

Builds a prognostic risk score (TimiRS) by LASSO-penalized Cox regression (glmnet, alpha = 1) on Cox-selected immune marker gene pairs (IMGPs), achieving Harrell's C-index 0.621-0.659 across five independent melanoma validation cohorts.

## When to use

Use when deriving a prognostic risk score from bulk tumor transcriptomes: fit LASSO (glmnet alpha=1) Cox regression on Cox-preselected marker gene pairs (e.g., top 21 CD8-Tem IMGPs) and validate across independent survival cohorts.

## When NOT to use

- No matched survival data or too few events: TimiGP requires a large gene expression dataset with matched survival information to identify prognostic gene pairs, and survival-analysis power depends on sample size and event number
- You need absolute cell-type abundance estimates from deconvolution (e.g., xCell or CIBERSORTx) rather than a relative prognostic risk score built from marker-pair orderings
- Your features are raw expression values or an unfiltered gene set: this skill operates on the binary MPS matrix of Cox-preselected IMGPs, not on unselected expression or already-computed abundance tables

## Inputs

- Bulk transcriptomic profiles of the training cohort (TCGA SKCM06 RNA) and validation cohorts (GSE65904, GSE22155, GSE54467, GSE53118, GSE8401)
- Matched clinical survival data (time-to-event and event status), pre-filtered with TimiCheckEvent
- Marker Pair Score (MPS) matrix generated by TimiGenePair from the CD8-Tem marker list (1 = marker A expression > marker B)
- Cox-selected prognostic IMGPs from TimiCOX on the training cohort, restricted to the top 21 CD8-Tem-derived IMGPs

## Outputs

- TimiRS LASSO coefficient vector (risk-score weights for the selected IMGPs)
- Per-patient TimiRS risk scores for each validation cohort
- Harrell's C-index per cohort (expected range 0.621-0.659) and TimiRS-control comparison values
- Median-split high/low-risk group assignments with Kaplan-Meier log-rank results per cell-cell interaction
- Summary table (e.g., timirs_validation_results.csv) assembling C-index values, control comparison, and stratification results

## How to apply

Preprocess each cohort with TimiPrePropress (log transformation plus gene-wise median normalization over the CD8-Tem marker list) and filter clinical data with TimiCheckEvent, then convert expression to a binary Marker Pair Score (MPS) matrix with TimiGenePair (1 = marker A expression > marker B). On the training cohort (TCGA SKCM06 metastatic melanoma), run TimiCOX univariate Cox regression to screen marker pairs, restrict features to the top 21 CD8-Tem-derived IMGPs, and fit a LASSO penalized Cox model with glmnet (alpha = 1, family = 'cox') to obtain the TimiRS risk-score coefficients — the rationale, per the article, is that focusing on marker gene pairs associated with cell-cell interactions yields an interpretable and accurate model. Apply the training-cohort coefficients frozen to each validation cohort's MPS matrix (GSE65904, GSE22155, GSE54467, GSE53118, GSE8401), and compute Harrell's C-index per cohort; also fit a TimiRS-control model from marker pairs unrelated to the 19 enriched cell-cell interactions for comparison. Finally, split patients at the median risk score per cohort and confirm Kaplan-Meier/log-rank separation of overall survival.

## Examples

```
library(glmnet); fit <- cv.glmnet(x = t(mps[, imgps_top21]), y = Surv(info$time, info$event), family = 'cox', alpha = 1)
```

## Evaluation signals

- TimiRS C-index lies within the reported 0.621-0.659 range across all five validation cohorts (GSE65904, GSE22155, GSE54467, GSE53118, GSE8401)
- TimiRS C-index exceeds the TimiRS-control model C-index (control built from marker pairs not associated with the 19 enriched cell-cell interactions)
- Median-split high/low-risk groups show significant Kaplan-Meier/log-rank separation of overall survival in all five datasets for the 19 cell-cell interactions
- LASSO is fit only on Cox-selected pairs (TimiCOX screen) restricted to the top 21 IMGPs with alpha = 1, and coefficients are applied (not refit) to validation MPS matrices
- Every cohort used in scoring was preprocessed identically with TimiPrePropress (log transformation + gene-wise median normalization) on the same CD8-Tem marker list

## Limitations

- TimiGP requires a large gene expression dataset with matched survival information to identify prognostic gene pairs; power depends on sample size, number of events, and quality of survival information
- Cell-cell interaction identification is enrichment-based, so cell types with fewer marker genes are less likely to be identified and contribute pairs to the model
- No wet-lab experiments were performed to validate the important findings, raising uncertainty about the reliability of the inferred cell-cell interactions underlying the model features
- Coefficients are learned once on the training cohort (TCGA SKCM06) and applied frozen; the C-index range 0.621-0.659 is cohort- and validation-set-dependent
