# clinvar-variant-gene-mapping

## Summary

Maps SpliceAI-prioritized ClinVar VUSs to their harboring genes (matched to GENCODE v19 gene symbols/Ensembl IDs) and joins each gene to MRSD score tables from four GTEx biosamples to quantify what fraction of splice-impacting VUSs are potentially RNA-seq assayable (up to 5,086/7,507; 67.8%).

## When to use

Use when you have ClinVar VUSs prioritized by a splicing predictor (e.g., SpliceAI) and per-gene MRSD scores (.results.mrsd.txt) from whole blood, LCL, skeletal muscle, and cultured fibroblast GTEx datasets, and need the fraction of variants in low-MRSD (assayable) genes.

## When NOT to use

- The MRSD score tables (.results.mrsd.txt) for the four biosamples have not yet been generated by MRSDexplorer.py from junction counts and sequencing depths — generate scores first.
- The variant set is not ClinVar VUSs prioritized as splice-impacting (e.g., pathogenic/likely-pathogenic variants, or VUSs without SpliceAI splicing impact), since the 7,507-gene denominator and 25.8%-67.8% range are defined for that cohort.
- Genes cannot be cross-referenced to GENCODE v19 symbols/Ensembl IDs, so they will not match the MRSDexplorer identifier scheme used in the score tables.

## Inputs

- ClinVar variants of uncertain significance predicted by SpliceAI to impact splicing, with their harboring genes
- One-per-line gene list of 7,507 genes, as GENCODE v19 gene symbols and/or Ensembl gene IDs (two-column format)
- MRSD score tables (.results.mrsd.txt) for whole blood, LCL, skeletal muscle, and cultured fibroblast GTEx-derived datasets (--number_reads 8, --sj_prop 0.75, --mrsd_param 0.95, --read_type unique)

## Outputs

- Per-variant classification table (mrsd_variant_fraction.csv) flagging each VUS's gene as low-MRSD (<= 100 M reads) or high-MRSD in each biosample
- Final fraction of SpliceAI-prioritized ClinVar VUSs in low-MRSD genes, expected to fall in the 25.8%-67.8% range across model stringency settings

## How to apply

Generate or load MRSD score tables (.results.mrsd.txt) for the four biosamples computed by MRSDexplorer.py at --number_reads 8, --sj_prop 0.75, --mrsd_param 0.95, --read_type unique. Build the one-per-line gene list of the 7,507 SpliceAI-filtered ClinVar VUS-harboring genes, formatted with up to two columns (GENCODE v19 gene symbol and/or Ensembl gene ID) matching the MRSDexplorer genes-file convention. In Python (pandas), join each variant's gene to its MRSD value in each biosample, treating '-' entries as genes whose median splice-junction coverage was 0 reads (not assayable). Flag each gene as low-MRSD if its MRSD is <= 100 million reads in at least one biosample, then compute the fraction of variants in low-MRSD genes, stratifying across model stringency settings to yield the 25.8%-67.8% range. Write the per-variant classification table and final fraction to mrsd_variant_fraction.csv.

## Examples

```
import pandas as pd; mrsd = pd.read_csv('mrsd.results.mrsd.txt', sep='\t', skiprows=12); v = pd.merge(variants, mrsd, left_on='gene_symbol', right_on='id1'); v['low_mrsd'] = v[['blood','lcl','muscle','fibroblast']].le(100).any(axis=1)
```

## Evaluation signals

- The computed fraction of VUSs in low-MRSD genes must reproduce the reported 25.8%-67.8% range, with the 67.8% upper bound (5,086/7,507) corresponding to the least stringent model setting in at least one biosample.
- Every '-' MRSD entry is interpreted as median splice-junction coverage of 0 reads (gene not assayable) and is counted as low-MRSD, not dropped or treated as missing.
- Gene identifiers in the variant table and the MRSD results cross-reference cleanly against the GENCODE v19 symbol/Ensembl ID two-column scheme; unmatched genes are explicitly accounted for rather than silently discarded.
- Per-variant output table (mrsd_variant_fraction.csv) contains one row per VUS with low/high-MRSD flags for each of the four biosamples (whole blood, LCL, skeletal muscle, fibroblast).
- The low-MRSD threshold is consistently applied as <= 100 million reads in at least one of the four biosamples at 8-read coverage.

## Limitations

- MRSD scores are derived from GTEx Illumina 75 bp paired-end poly-A enrichment RNA-seq; pre-computed scores are most accurate for similar workflows, and other sequencing methodologies require regenerating scores with MRSDexplorer.
- The MRSD model is validated only for bulk short-read poly-A enrichment RNA-seq processed with a specific bioinformatics pipeline; long-read, single-cell, and spatially resolved RNA-seq are not covered.
- MRSD does not explicitly account for eQTLs/sQTLs, genomic background transcriptome-wide changes (e.g., interferonopathies, spliceosome disruption), or allele-specific expression and differential expression applications.

- [readme] Gene list file format for MRSDexplorer matching variants to MRSD scores: "Where analysis of genes, rather than transcripts, is desired, users can provide a text file containing a list of genes of interest, one per line."
- [readme] Interpretation of '-' MRSD entries in the score table: "Where '-' is given as a required read depth, median coverage of splice junctions for the given gene was 0 reads."
