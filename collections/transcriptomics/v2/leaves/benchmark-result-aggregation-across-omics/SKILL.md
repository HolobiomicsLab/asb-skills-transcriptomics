# benchmark-result-aggregation-across-omics

## Summary

Aggregates perturbation-benchmark outputs (regulator activity score matrices from 11 inference methods) across transcriptomics and phospho-proteomics collections into cross-method agreement statistics: median pairwise Spearman correlations of activity scores and median Jaccard indexes on top-5% regulator sets, as reported for Fig. 1B of the decoupleR manuscript.

## When to use

Use when you have per-method activity score matrices from decoupleR benchmark runs (e.g. run_rna_bench.R / run_php_bench.R outputs on Zenodo record 5645208) and need to quantify whether different inference methods produce similar regulator activity estimates.

## When NOT to use

- You are evaluating predictive performance of methods against perturbation ground truth (AUROC/AUPRC with downsampling) — use the decoupleRBench evaluation pipeline instead of agreement aggregation
- You only have a single method's activity scores and no cross-method panel to compare
- Input is raw count tables/metadata for the benchmark itself, not the inferred activity score matrices

## Inputs

- Per-method regulator activity score matrices from the transcriptomics benchmark (run_rna_bench.R)
- Per-method regulator activity score matrices from the phospho-proteomics benchmark (run_php_bench.R)
- Benchmark data retrieved from Zenodo record 5645208

## Outputs

- method_agreement_metrics.csv with four statistics: median Spearman correlation (transcriptomics and phospho-proteomics) and median Jaccard index (transcriptomics and phospho-proteomics)
- Optional Fig. 1B-style cross-method agreement heatmap

## How to apply

Load the per-method regulator activity score matrices generated by the transcriptomics benchmark (run_rna_bench.R) and phospho-proteomics benchmark (run_php_bench.R), retrieving the underlying data from Zenodo record 5645208 (via Rscript R/process/get_data.R). For each omics collection, compute all pairwise Spearman rank correlations of activity scores between the 11 methods and take the median across method pairs; separately, for each method rank regulators by activity score, retain the top 5% regulators, compute pairwise Jaccard indexes on those top-5% sets across methods, and take the median. Write the four statistics (median Spearman: transcriptomics and phospho-proteomics; median Jaccard: transcriptomics and phospho-proteomics) to method_agreement_metrics.csv, and optionally render a Fig. 1B-style agreement heatmap. The rationale is that methods may have differing activity distributions yet still agree in regulator rankings, so rank-based correlation and set-overhead overlap on top-ranked regulators capture agreement independent of score scale.

## Related tools

- **decoupleR** (Source of the 11 activity inference methods whose per-regulator activity score matrices are aggregated for the cross-method agreement statistics) — https://www.bioconductor.org/packages/release/bioc/html/decoupleR.html
- **R** (Implementation language for the benchmark scripts (run_rna_bench.R, run_php_bench.R) and the Spearman/Jaccard aggregation) — https://www.bioconductor.org/packages/release/bioc/html/decoupleR.html
- **decoupleRBench** (Benchmark R package that builds on decoupleR's decouple() wrapper to generate the per-method activities being aggregated) — https://github.com/saezlab/decoupleRBench

## Examples

```
Rscript R/process/run_rna_bench.R; Rscript R/process/run_php_bench.R
```

## Evaluation signals

- Reproduce the reported median Spearman correlation of activities between methods of 0.52 for transcriptomics and 0.65 for phospho-proteomics
- Reproduce the reported median Jaccard indexes of 0.23 (transcriptomics) and 0.21 (phospho-proteomics)
- Verify method_agreement_metrics.csv contains exactly four statistics covering both omics collections and both metric families
- Confirm 11 methods are present in each activity score matrix before computing pairwise correlations
- Heatmap, if rendered, matches the Fig. 1B agreement layout of the manuscript

## Limitations

- Activity distributions differ across methods, so raw score-scale comparisons are uninformative; only rank-based (Spearman) and top-5% set-overlap (Jaccard) summaries are meaningful
- Noise and scalability analyses (run_rna_noise.R ~6 hours, run_php_noise.R ~2 hours) are separate workflows and not covered by this aggregation step
- Findings reflect the decoupleR manuscript's transcriptomic and phospho-proteomic perturbation benchmarks; agreement values may differ on other omics collections
