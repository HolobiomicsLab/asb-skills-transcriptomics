# transposable-element-read-simulation-benchmarking

> **License: noncommercial** — confirm your use is a permitted (noncommercial) purpose before applying; commercial use requires a separate license (see `metadata.tool_license`). <!-- asb-license-banner -->
## Summary

Benchmark locus-specific TE quantification accuracy by generating synthetic single-cell read datasets with known ground truth (Polyester) and scoring MATES against baselines (scTE, SoloTE) on locus-level R2, read recapture rate, and isoform counts.

## When to use

Use when you need to quantify how accurately a TE quantification method recovers locus-specific abundances by comparing against simulated reads with known ground-truth locus expression, e.g., simulated ~300 bp Alu repeats.

## When NOT to use

- The question is TE quantification on real data where no ground-truth locus abundances exist — use downstream biological validation (e.g., long-read scNanoRNA-seq or PacBio) instead of simulation
- The target TE is a long autonomously expressed element such as L1HS spanning several thousand bases, which the MATES 2001-bp coverage vector design cannot fully capture
- The goal is subfamily-level-only TE quantification where multi-mapping read allocation is irrelevant

## Inputs

- Simulated single-cell read datasets (BAM) generated by Polyester with known ground-truth locus abundances
- Ground-truth locus abundance tables for 6 Alu subfamilies, 15 simple-repeat subfamilies, and HERVK-int TE1/TE2 isoforms
- TE reference (RepeatMasker-derived BED) and flanking genomic context for coverage-vector construction
- 100-bp short-read simulations modeled on scNanoRNA-seq data

## Outputs

- simulation_benchmark_results.csv containing locus-level R2, per-method percent of simulated reads captured, and HERVK TE1/TE2 isoform counts
- Per-locus TE count matrices from MATES, scTE, and SoloTE on identical simulated BAMs

## How to apply

Run Polyester to simulate single-cell reads with known ground-truth locus abundances — 6 Alu subfamilies (~300 bp), 15 simple-repeat subfamilies, the HERVK-int isoform pair TE1/TE2, and 100-bp reads modeled on scNanoRNA-seq data. Process the simulated BAMs with the MATES package (bam_processor to split unique- vs multi-mapping reads, data_processor to build 2001-bp coverage vectors centered on TE start positions), then run MATES_model's TE_quantifier for short reads and TE_quantifier_LongRead for the 100-bp reads. Run scTE and SoloTE on the identical simulated BAM files as baselines. Score every method against the Polyester ground truth by locus-level R2 correlation, percentage of simulated reads captured, and HERVK TE1/TE2 isoform counts, writing all metrics to simulation_benchmark_results.csv. The rationale is that simulation provides the only setting where per-locus truth is known, enabling direct comparison of how methods allocate multi-mapping reads.

## Evaluation signals

- MATES reaches locus-level R2 = 0.7420 against the simulated Alu repeat ground truth
- MATES captures on average 96.14% of simulated reads vs 92.57% recaptured by scTE
- Simple-repeat locus-level R2 = 0.7479 and HERVK TE1/TE2 counts of 4140/1295 against ground truth 4126/1282
- Long-read (100-bp simulated) locus-level R2 = 0.4923 vs SoloTE strategy R2 = 0.1178
- All metrics written to simulation_benchmark_results.csv comparing methods on identical simulated BAM files

## Limitations

- Long-read simulation shows substantially lower locus-level R2 (0.4923), indicating reduced accuracy on this data type
- Isoforms with high sequence similarity and no uniquely mappable flanking regions (e.g., HERVK isoforms) remain hard to distinguish
- Estimated multi-mapping rates may be inaccurate for TEs longer than 2001 bp due to incomplete flanking coverage
