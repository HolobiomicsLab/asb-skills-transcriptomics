# statistical-agreement-reproducibility-checking

## Summary

Reproduces cross-method agreement statistics from the decoupleR benchmarks by computing pairwise Spearman correlations and top-5% Jaccard indexes of inferred regulator activities across the 11 methods, validating against the published medians (Spearman 0.52 transcriptomics / 0.65 phospho-proteomics; Jaccard 0.23 / 0.21).

## When to use

Use when you have per-method regulator activity score matrices from multiple inference methods (e.g. decoupleR on transcriptomics or phospho-proteomics benchmarks) and need to quantify whether methods agree.

## When NOT to use

- The goal is to evaluate predictive accuracy (AUROC/AUPRC) of methods against known perturbations — use the decoupleRBench evaluation pipeline with downsampling instead of agreement statistics.
- Only a single inference method's activities are available; pairwise Spearman and Jaccard comparisons require at least two methods.
- Input is raw count data before activity inference — activities must first be computed via the decoupleR 'decouple' wrapper.

## Inputs

- Per-method regulator activity score matrices from the transcriptomics benchmark (run_rna_bench.R)
- Per-method regulator activity score matrices from the phospho-proteomics benchmark (run_php_bench.R)
- Benchmark data from Zenodo record 5645208 (count tables and metadata used by decoupleRBench)

## Outputs

- method_agreement_metrics.csv with median Spearman (transcriptomics, phospho-proteomics) and median Jaccard (transcriptomics, phospho-proteomics)
- Optional Fig. 1B-style cross-method agreement heatmap

## How to apply

Load the per-method activity score matrices generated by run_rna_bench.R and run_php_bench.R (decoupleRBench pipeline), retrieving underlying data from Zenodo record 5645208 via Rscript R/process/get_data.R. For each omics collection, compute all pairwise Spearman rank correlations of activity scores between the 11 methods and take the median across method pairs. Then, per method, rank regulators by activity score, retain the top 5% regulators, compute pairwise Jaccard indexes on the top-5% sets across methods, and take the median. Write the four statistics to method_agreement_metrics.csv and optionally render a Fig. 1B-style agreement heatmap; the skill is applied correctly only if the computed medians reproduce the reported values (Spearman 0.52 and 0.65; Jaccard 0.23 and 0.21).

## Evaluation signals

- Median pairwise Spearman correlation between methods is 0.52 for transcriptomics and 0.65 for phospho-proteomics
- Median pairwise Jaccard index on top-5% regulator sets is 0.23 for transcriptomics and 0.21 for phospho-proteomics
- method_agreement_metrics.csv contains exactly four statistics: median Spearman and median Jaccard for each omics collection
- All pairwise comparisons cover the 11 decoupleR methods; the number of method pairs equals C(11,2) = 55 per omics collection
- Spearman correlations remain positive and moderately high even though the methods show differing activity distributions

## Limitations

- Methods show differing activity distributions, so agreement is assessed via rank correlation (Spearman) and set overlap (Jaccard on top 5%) rather than raw score comparison.
- The cross-method agreement analysis supplements, but does not replace, AUROC/AUPRC benchmark evaluation, where true positive classes are limited by regulators covered in perturbation experiments and require downsampling.
- The noise and scalability analyses (run_rna_noise.R ~6 hours, run_php_noise.R ~2 hours) are computationally expensive and are separate from this agreement task.
