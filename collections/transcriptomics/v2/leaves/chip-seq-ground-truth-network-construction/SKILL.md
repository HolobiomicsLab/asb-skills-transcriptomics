# chip-seq-ground-truth-network-construction

## Summary

Constructs directed, binary gene-by-gene ground-truth networks from ChIP-seq and perturb-seq supplementary data (e.g. MCalla et al. human/mouse ESC intersection gold standards and the K562 genome-wide perturb-seq p-value matrix) so that GRN inference methods can be benchmarked with EPR and AUPRC metrics via the BenGRN/GRnnData packages.

## When to use

Use when benchmarking GRN inference methods (scPRINT, scGPT, DeepSEM, Geneformer, GENIE3) against cell type-specific ChIP-seq or perturb-seq ground truths such as MCalla et al. ESC networks or K562 gwps.

## When NOT to use

- When only mock-up expression data generated by ODE simulators (e.g. SERGIO) is available and no real orthogonal sequencing ground truth exists — the article notes many GN methods are 'unfortunately, benchmarked on ODE-generated mock-up expression data'
- When a cell-type-specific causal truth is required but only literature-based collections like Omnipath are available, which 'did not improve performance in most of our tests'
- When ground truth orientation is unknown — mis-transposing the matrix (targets/regulators convention) invalidates all EPR/AUPRC comparisons

## Inputs

- MCalla et al. supplementary gold-standard directed binary networks (source-to-target lists) for two human and two mouse ESC datasets
- Yan ESC expression dataset E-GEOD-36552 from the gene expression atlas (https://www.ebi.ac.uk/gxa/sc/experiments/E-GEOD-36552/downloads)
- Genome-wide perturb-seq (gwps) multiple-hypothesis-testing-corrected p-value matrix for K562
- Inferred GRN score matrices from methods such as scPRINT, scGPT, DeepSEM, Geneformer v2, GENIE3

## Outputs

- Directed binary gene-by-gene ground-truth networks (rows = targets, columns = regulators)
- esc_benchmark_results.csv with AUPRC and EPR for every method-dataset combination
- gwps_benchmark_metrics.csv with per-method AUPRC and EPR values

## How to apply

Download the ground truth from supplementary sources: the MCalla et al. intersection gold-standard directed networks (plus the Yan E-GEOD-36552 ESC expression data from the gene expression atlas) or the gwps multiple-hypothesis-testing-corrected p-value matrix for K562. Convert source-to-target lists into a gene-by-gene binary graph, or binarize the gwps matrix with a cutoff of 0.05 on corrected p-values over the 8000+ expressed K562 genes. Match inferred networks to the ground truth respecting the convention that row elements are targets and column elements are regulators, using the non-transposed orientation for ChIP-seq/perturb-seq ground truths. Optionally select attention heads for scPRINT against the ground truth with scikit-learn's RidgeClassifier (L2 penalty set to 1, positivity constraint on coefficients, no intercept). Compute EPR as the diagnostic odds ratio (TP x TN)/(FP x FN) at the cutoff yielding K positive predictions (K = number of ground-truth positives) and AUPRC floored at positive-class prevalence without interpolation to perfect recall, assembling results into CSV tables (esc_benchmark_results.csv, gwps_benchmark_metrics.csv).

## Related tools

- **BenGRN** (Benchmarking package used to compute EPR and AUPRC of inferred networks against the constructed ChIP-seq/perturb-seq ground truths) — https://github.com/jkobject/benGRN
- **GRnnData** (Package for representing and matching gene regulatory networks against the ground truth during evaluation) — https://github.com/cantinilab/GRnnData
- **scikit-learn** (RidgeClassifier (L2 penalty 1, positive coefficients, no intercept) for selecting attention heads against the ground-truth edges)
- **scPRINT** (Primary method whose inferred networks are matched and scored against the constructed ground truths) — https://github.com/cantinilab/scPRINT

## Evaluation signals

- Results table contains AUPRC and EPR entries for every method-dataset combination (all five methods on two human and two mouse ESC datasets, or on K562 gwps)
- EPR near 1 indicates random edge recovery; values above 1 indicate better-than-random recovery of gold-standard edges
- gwps binarization at the 0.05 corrected-p-value cutoff yields a directed network covering all 8000+ expressed K562 genes
- Network orientation verified: row elements are targets and column elements are regulators, using the non-transposed orientation for ChIP-seq/perturb-seq ground truths
- AUPRC area floored at the positive-class prevalence and computed without interpolation to perfect recall

## Limitations

- No perfect ground truths exist for gene network inference benchmarking; ChIP-seq, perturb-seq, and literature-based ground truths remain scarce and ambiguous
- MCalla et al. gold standards cover only two human and two mouse ESC datasets; gwps covers only the K562 cell line, limiting generalization
- The head-selection variant of scPRINT is trained against the ground truth itself (RidgeClassifier on ground-truth edges) and is not fully zero-shot
